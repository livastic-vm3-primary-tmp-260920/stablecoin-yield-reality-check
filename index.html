What is measured — and what is not

  • Base / rewards / total APY: the third-party feed's apyBase / apyReward / apy; reward split is explicitly unknown if missing, never silently replaced with zero.
  • 30-day average and 7-day change: provider-reported apyMean30d and apyPct7D; these are retrospective estimates, not forward guarantees.
  • Incentive-heavy flag: rewards ≥ 30% of headline APY. Large weekly move: absolute 7d change ≥ 3 percentage points or 60% of headline APY. Flags are neither audits nor actuarial probability estimates.
  • No risk evaluation: token depeg, bad debt, oracle manipulation, smart-contract code and governance, withdrawal restrictions, fees/slippage and legal/regulatory considerations are NOT verified.
  • Educational explanation: How a hypothetical 6% APY can still lose money after $12 of fees, with fully disclosed AI assistance and no wallet interaction.
  • Open, reproducible code: GitHub source, test suite and timestamped local snapshot. The free API is scheduled to change November 13, 2026; after its announced sunset, refreshing fails closed rather than misrepresenting stale data.
